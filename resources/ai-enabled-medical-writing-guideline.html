SECTION 01 · RATIONALE

Executive Rationale & Core Principle

🎯 Operational Rationale
  • Execution Accelerator: Artificial Intelligence is a powerful tool to enhance productivity, accelerate document drafting, and support QC review.
  • Human Integrity: AI does NOT replace professional judgment, regulatory expertise, or final author accountability.
  • Standardization: Establishes firm operational boundaries across HiRO Medical Writing operations.
⚖️ Foundational Golden Rule
AI Accelerates.
Humans Decide.
Humans Own.

AI supports efficiency and execution. Medical Writers remain fully responsible for scientific judgment, regulatory strategy, and final deliverables.

PRINCIPLE 01 / 06

1. Human in the Loop

🤖 What AI May Assist
  • Initial draft generation & scaffolding
  • Scientific literature summarization
  • Data organization & structural formatting
  • Cross-document consistency checking
  • Quality control support & verification
🛡️ Mandatory Human Gate
  • Content shared directly with sponsors / clients
  • Content included in regulated regulatory dossiers
  • Content submitted for formal approval
  • Content incorporated into final deliverables
🚨 Core Mandate: No AI-generated content shall be accepted or released without critical human evaluation.

PRINCIPLE 04 / 06

4. Evidence Before Generation

✅ Approved Source Grounding
  • Approved study protocols & CSR documents
  • Sponsor-verified source documents & data
  • Peer-reviewed scientific literature
  • Applicable SOPs & ICH/FDA/EMA guidance
  • Verified trial data & clinical registries
🚫 Strictly Unacceptable Practices
  • ❌ Fabricated data: Invented trial numbers
  • ❌ Fabricated references: Hallucinated citations
  • ❌ Invented regulations: Fake regulatory rules
  • ❌ Unsupported claims: Speculative statements
🔍 Core Directive: Trust, but verify. Source information must always take absolute precedence over AI assumptions.

PRINCIPLE 05 / 06

5. No AI Output Without Quality Control

AI outputs must always be treated as working drafts requiring verification, never final deliverables. Assess three critical pillars:

1️⃣ Factual Accuracy
  • Numerical data & tables
  • Statistical values & p-values
  • References & citations
  • Scientific statements
2️⃣ Consistency
  • Cross-document alignment
  • Medical terminology standards
  • Study design logic
  • Dosing & cohort fidelity
3️⃣ Reg Compliance
  • Regulatory expectations
  • Internal SOP standards
  • Sponsor specific guidelines
  • ICH-GCP regulatory rules

PRINCIPLE 06 / 06

6. Think Company Policy First

🔒 Institutional Compliance Pillars
  • Full alignment with official Company SOPs & Policies
  • Strict protection of Confidential Information & IP
  • Rigorous adherence to Sponsor NDAs & Agreements
  • Compliance with Data Privacy & GDPR/HIPAA
  • Execution of mandatory Quality Procedures
⚠️ Uncertainty Protocol

When uncertainty exists regarding AI tool compliance, data safety, or confidentiality, team members must seek guidance prior to tool invocation.

"If a task cannot be performed in compliance with company policy, it should NOT be performed using AI."

SECTION 03

AI Capabilities & Boundaries

What AI Can vs. Cannot Replace
Section 3 delineates AI capabilities vs irreplaceable human medical judgment.
CONFIDENTIAL
CAPABILITY MATRIX

What AI Can vs. Cannot Replace

⚡ AI Can Enhance (Execution Speed)
  • 🚀 Draft creation: Rapid scaffolding of document outlines
  • 📄 Summarization: Concise synthesis of dense literature
  • 🔍 Information extraction: Accelerated data parsing
  • 📐 Formatting support: Layout & style standardization
  • 🔍 QC assistance: Automated consistency checking
  • 📝 Administrative tasks: Routine writing & formatting
🧠 AI Cannot Replace (Human Expertise)
  • 🩺 Medical judgment: Specialized clinical reasoning
  • 🏛️ Regulatory strategy: Complex agency negotiation
  • 💡 Critical thinking: Logical interrogation & questioning
  • 🔬 Scientific interpretation: Deep data contextualization
  • ⚖️ Risk-benefit assessment: Safety evaluation
  • 🤝 Client consultation & decision-making: Executive trust
