02 / HOW WE WORK

Six principles for medical writing

Use this standard whenever AI, source evidence, review, and final deliverables meet.

01 / 06

Human in the Loop

AI may assist drafting, summarization, structuring, and checking; a human gate is mandatory before content is shared or submitted.

No AI-generated content is released without critical human evaluation.
02 / 06

Human Owns the Outcome

The assigned Medical Writer retains accountability for scientific accuracy, regulatory compliance, document quality, conclusions, and sponsor communication.

AI errors never transfer responsibility away from the document owner.
03 / 06

Use the Best LLM for Each Task

Match model capability to task complexity: extraction, synthesis, writing, quality checks, and verification may require different tools.

Use the best model for the task, not one model for everything.
04 / 06

Evidence Before Generation

Approved protocols, CSRs, sponsor-verified data, literature, SOPs, guidance, registries, and other verified sources outrank model assumptions.

Trust, but verify. Source information takes precedence.
05 / 06

No AI Output Without Quality Control

Treat AI output as working draft material and verify factual accuracy, cross-document consistency, medical terminology, and regulatory alignment.

Every draft passes the three-pillar QC check before release.
06 / 06

Think Company Policy First

Follow company SOPs, confidentiality and IP controls, sponsor agreements, data privacy requirements, and mandatory quality procedures.

When compliance is uncertain, pause and seek guidance before tool use.
Core mandateAI accelerates execution; human medical expertise holds scientific authority, judgement, and release accountability.
